Architecture
U-FNO-LR retains the original six Fourier layers, width32, modes8, original
spectral convolutions, activations, projection head and last-three-layer U-Net
placement. Replace only its U-Net branches with two stride-two encoder levels
(20->10->5), BN/LeakyReLU encoder blocks, and bilinear size-matched decoding
with convolution and skip concatenation. No transposed convolution or cropping.
This is a combined depth/decoder adaptation, not an isolated mechanism test.

scale1. Other fields use their actual std (do not add a dimensionally inappropriate
1e-10 offset to permeability measured near1e-14). Preserve parameters in JSON.

Training budget, fixed before training

Gradient clipping norm1 after backward. Float32 training.
Each epoch uses16 distinct uniformly drawn transitions per training realisation,
